Privacy Policy – MileTune Car Radio Online

Application: MileTune Car Radio Online (com.miletune.onlineradio)
Contact: serverx2004@gmail.com
Last updated: November 23, 2026

Summary: MileTune Car Radio Online does not require an account, does not display advertisements, and does not sell personal data. Preferences and trial/PRO state are stored locally on your device, while PRO purchases are processed through Google Play Billing.

1. Information We Collect and How It Is Used

  • Local app preferences and state: Settings such as selected language, display/theme preferences, favorite stations/presets, trial status, and local PRO entitlement state are stored locally on your device so the app works smoothly across sessions.
  • Google Play Billing: If you purchase or restore miletune_pro_lifetime, payment processing, purchase verification, and refunds/revocations are handled by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details.
  • Audio streaming: When you play an online radio station, your device connects directly to the public audio stream URL provided for that station. Standard network metadata (such as your IP address and connection headers) is transmitted to reach the stream source.

2. Ads, Analytics, and Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell personal data to third parties and we do not run ad networks inside the app. External radio streams are operated by their respective broadcasters or stream providers and are subject to their own network availability and terms.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you clear the app's storage/data in Android settings or uninstall the app. Purchases made through Google Play are managed under your Google account in Google Play.

4. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or the app, contact us at serverx2004@gmail.com.

  • Preferințe și stare locală: Opțiuni precum limba selectată, tema/modul de afișare, posturile favorite/preset-urile, starea perioadei de probă și starea locală PRO sunt salvate local pe dispozitiv pentru funcționarea aplicației.
